Self-Balancing Inertial Screens (GISL)

For bulk materials with bulk density up to 1.4 t/m³.

Request Information →

Self-balancing inertial screens are designed for screening bulk materials with bulk density up to 1.4 t/m³. They are used in dewatering, desliming, screening of coal, anthracite and oil shale, as well as for the separation of suspensions from beneficiation products. For specific material categories, reinforced GSS screens are produced (thickened sides and liners, thicker seamless tubes, reinforced bearings).

Key Features

  • Bolted vibrating box construction
  • 2 in-house inertial vibrators
  • Direct (coupling) torque transmission
  • Transverse, longitudinal or wedge screen tensioning system
  • Guards on rotating parts (vibrator unbalance weights)
  • Vibration-resistant heavy-duty bearings
  • Centralised grease lubrication system
  • Heavy-duty springs
  • Adjustable oscillation amplitude
  • Sides and liners made of 09G2S or 10KhSND steel
  • Rubber-lined feed chute

Optional equipment

  • Integrated washing system with nozzles for each deck
  • Sliding gates on discharge chutes
  • Dust-proof enclosure
  • Wear-resistant rubber lining of beams, discharge chutes and sides
  • Removable sub-frames for screen replacement (transverse, longitudinal, wedge mounting)
