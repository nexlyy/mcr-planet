High-Frequency Screens

Dry/wet screening, dewatering, desliming and washing of suspensions.

Request Information →

High-frequency screens are intended for dry or wet screening of bulk materials with bulk density not exceeding 2.8 t/m³, as well as for desliming, dewatering and washing of suspensions from beneficiation products. They can be used for screening sand, ores and a wide range of other materials.

Key Features

  • Bolted vibrating box construction
  • High-efficiency twin motor-vibrators
  • Modular tensioning system for various screen types
  • Inner surfaces protected with wear-resistant lining
  • Heavy-duty rubber shock absorbers used instead of springs — significantly improve vibration efficiency and ensure maximum screening or dewatering at minimum energy consumption
  • Adjustable amplitude
  • Robust construction resistant to vibration loads
  • Additional dewatering area at the pulp feed point
  • Removable sub-frame for screen replacement

Optional equipment

  • Integrated washing system with nozzles for each deck
  • Slotted, polyurethane or rubber screens
  • Deck split into two streams to dewater products of different size
  • Dust-proof enclosure
