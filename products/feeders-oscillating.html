Oscillating Feeders

Continuous-transport equipment with reciprocating tray.

Request Information →

Specialised continuous-transport equipment whose main working part is a tray of special design performing reciprocating motion. It is intended for uniform discharge of non-sticky bulk materials from hoppers and other containers into process machines or conveying devices. Such feeders are installed directly under receiving funnels or hoppers. The oscillating feeder features a relatively simple design, ease of repair and maintenance, and high operating reliability.

Key Features

  • Bolted vibrating box construction
  • Inner surfaces protected with wear-resistant plates
  • Replaceable grate bars of various types
  • Heavy-duty spring shock absorbers
  • Vibrating motors from a reliable European manufacturer
  • Smooth oscillation frequency control
  • Adjustable oscillation amplitude
  • Material feed rate adjustment
