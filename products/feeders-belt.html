Belt Feeders

Transport of bulk raw materials from hoppers to main equipment.

Request Information →

Belt feeders are designed for the transport (movement) of bulk raw materials from hoppers to main process equipment. They handle wet and fine materials without clogging or jamming, with belt speed regulated by a frequency converter.

Key Features

  • Operation with wet and fine materials without clogging or jamming
  • Belt speed regulated by frequency converter
  • Adjustable cross-section for the discharged material
  • Variable belt widths
  • High reliability of mechanisms and assemblies
  • Available with own hopper or adapted to customer's hopper
  • Loading and discharge openings at different levels
  • Several belt feeders can be installed in one row
