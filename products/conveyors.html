Belt Conveyors

Continuous transport of bulk and unit loads — sand, gravel, cement, bricks.

Request Information →

Belt conveyors are the most widespread type of transporting machine, designed for the continuous flow of bulk materials (sand, soil, cement), lump materials (crushed stone, gravel and others) and unit loads (bricks, blocks, tiles and others) in horizontal and inclined positions. The power and dimensions of the manufactured conveyor depend on the area of application and operating conditions.

Key Features

  • Rigid welded frame (truss or channel-beam type)
  • Adjustable supports
  • Rubber-coated drive and idler drums
  • Effective polyurethane belt cleaners
  • Dust-protected lower idler rollers
  • Belt spliced to length at the factory and easily mounted on site
  • Full safety package: rope-pull stops, slip and tear sensors, emergency cutoff, mesh guards
  • Possibility of integration into a complete technological line
  • Inclination up to 18° (smooth belt) or 25° (chevron belt)
