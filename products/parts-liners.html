Liners, Throwers & Grates for Crushers

Wear parts for reliable crusher operation under heavy loads.

Request Information →

During operation, crushing equipment is subjected to constant loads and severe external impact due to the abrasiveness of the materials being crushed. It is therefore impossible to avoid wear of the equipment during use. The only way to maximally extend its operating life is to use wear parts made of special steels. We specialise in the manufacture and repair of wear parts for crushing-and-sorting equipment. One of our activities is the production of beaters, hammers, liners, throwers and grates for crushers. Manufacture of parts to customer samples or drawings is possible.
