Washing & Classification Equipment

Sieve-hydrocyclone modules: hydrocyclones, screen, slurry pump, sump.

Request Information →

Equipment for washing and beneficiation, including modules with hydrocyclones, a high-frequency screen, slurry pumps and sumps. The sieve-hydrocyclone module is a combination of a hydrocyclone group with a high-frequency dewatering GVCh screen, a slurry pump and a settling sump joined into a single system. The choice of hydrocyclone models and the number installed in the battery depends on the technological tasks regarding the quality and required size of sand fractions, the granulometric composition of the feed material, and the overall productivity of the beneficiation complex. The module can be installed either separately on a supporting steel structure (in which case the fine-fraction sand, after dewatering on the screen, is delivered to a separate cone) or directly above a classifier (in which case the fine-fraction sand is joined with the coarse-fraction sand from the classifier and the combined mass is delivered to a common cone).
